Indigenous & Regional Games

Southern Chile's Mapuche communities keep centuries-old games alive, honoring ancestral skill, teamwork, and cultural identity.

Palín (Chueca): A Mapuche Tradition

Palín, also known as chueca, is a traditional Mapuche team sport played with wooden sticks and a small ball on open fields. Matches carry deep spiritual and community significance, often accompanied by ceremonies celebrating harvest and cultural unity.

Regional Festival Games

Across northern and central Chile, local festivals feature games rooted in agricultural life, including sack races, tug-of-war, and horseback skill competitions. These events strengthen community bonds while celebrating harvests, patron saints, and regional identity.

Preserving Cultural Heritage

Efforts to protect indigenous games continue through:

  • School programs teaching Mapuche traditions
  • Community tournaments held during cultural festivals
  • Documentation projects recording game rules and history
