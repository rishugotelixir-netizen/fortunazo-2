About This Project

Why we built a guide to Chile's most popular games.

Our Mission

We aim to celebrate and document the games that bring Chileans together, preserving traditions while highlighting the country's fast-growing modern gaming culture.

What We Offer

Our guide covers traditional street games, popular video games, card and tabletop favorites, and indigenous games from across Chile. Each page explains history, rules, and cultural significance in clear, accessible language for curious readers everywhere.

Our Approach

We research each topic carefully, organizing content around history, gameplay, and cultural context so visitors can quickly find and understand what makes each game special.

Why Choose Us

  • Clear, well-organized guides for every category
  • Content covering both heritage and modern trends
  • Simple language accessible to all readers
