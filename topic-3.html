Card & Board Games

Around Chilean tables, card and dice games mix strategy, bluffing, and laughter during family reunions and social gatherings.

Truco: The Art of Bluffing

Truco is a fast-paced card game built on strategy and deception, played in pairs or teams using a Spanish deck. Players signal partners with subtle gestures, and matches often become loud, competitive social events full of friendly trash talk.

Cacho: Dice and Deception

Cacho, a dice game similar to Perudo, challenges players to bluff about hidden dice values under a cup. It rewards sharp probability instincts and confident bluffing, making it a popular choice at bars, parties, and casual weekend gatherings.

Other Household Favorites

Beyond truco and cacho, several games remain household staples:

  • Dominó — tile-matching strategy for two to four players
  • Naipe chileno — traditional Spanish-deck card games
  • Damas — Chilean-style checkers
