Video Games in Chile

From mobile phones to gaming PCs, Chile has embraced digital play, becoming one of Latin America's most active gaming markets.

Mobile Gaming Dominance

Smartphones are the primary gaming device for most Chileans. Battle royale titles and casual puzzle games attract millions of daily players, thanks to affordable data plans and widespread smartphone ownership across urban and rural regions alike.

Esports and Competitive Play

Santiago hosts growing esports tournaments for football simulators and multiplayer online battle arenas. Local teams compete regionally, and streaming platforms have turned talented Chilean players into recognizable figures within the broader Latin American esports community.

Favorite Genres

Chilean players gravitate toward several genres:

  • Football and sports simulations
  • Battle royale and shooter titles
  • Sandbox and building games
